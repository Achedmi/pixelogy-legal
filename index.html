How we make them

  1. One process, checked facts. Every video starts from a short brief of verified facts about one real event, and the narration only says what the brief supports.
  2. Animated in code. Our own studio tool draws each scene as pixel art and films it in several shots, with camera moves, like a tiny documentary.
  3. Narrated and scored. A narrator tells the story in short sentences, with captions on screen and an original electronic score underneath.
  4. Checked, then shared. Each finished video passes automatic checks for length, sound and captions, then our tool posts it to the Pixelogy accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
