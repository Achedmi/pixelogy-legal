Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Pixelogy does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from viewers. The only account connected to our publishing tool is Pixelogy's own account.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile of the Pixelogy TikTok account (scope user.info.basic): used only to confirm we are posting to the right account.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload, video.publish): used only to post Pixelogy's own videos to that same account.

Access tokens are stored securely on Pixelogy's own publishing systems and are never shared with anyone else.

Viewers and children

Our videos are made for kids and families. The app never accesses information about people who watch, like or comment on our videos, and we do not run ads, tracking or analytics through this app.

Deleting data

The account owner can disconnect the app at any time from TikTok's settings, which revokes all access.
